A trading engine you can watch think.

A limit order book with price-time priority, a market maker that quotes around inventory risk, an options pricer with a fitted volatility surface, a delta-hedging simulator and an ETF arbitrage engine. Every figure below is computed by the OCaml code in this repository, not typed in.

    02 · Market making

    Where a market maker's money comes from, and goes

    An Avellaneda-Stoikov quoter shares the book with background flow that includes informed traders. PnL splits exactly into spread captured, adverse selection and inventory, and the three parts sum to the total.

    03 · Options

    A volatility surface fitted slice by slice

    Noisy option prices are inverted to implied volatility, then an SVI curve is fitted to each expiry and checked for butterfly arbitrage. Dots are the quotes, the line is the fit.

    04 · Delta hedging

    Selling an option and hedging it, 252 times a year

    Sell a call at 20% volatility, then trade the stock to stay delta neutral. If the stock really moves at 20% the hedge is fair and its error shrinks like 1 over the square root of the number of trades. If it moves at a different volatility, the profit is set by gamma.

    05 · ETF arbitrage

    When an ETF drifts from its basket, and when that pays

    An ETF is worth the stocks inside it. Trade the gap only if it covers both spreads, the creation fee and the cost of borrowing. If you can't trade every stock, a least-squares hedge replicates the rest.

    06 · Risk

    What a short-gamma book can lose

    5,000 hedged paths where the stock is more volatile than the option priced in. Historical value-at-risk and expected shortfall, against the closed-form normal estimate.

    07 · How it is built

    Small pieces, each one checked

    Plain OCaml with dune. Prices are integer ticks, so the engine never touches floating point. Every module has expect tests, and the statistical claims on this page are tests too: they fail if the maths is wrong.

    All data is simulated. This is a research and engineering project, not trading advice.
